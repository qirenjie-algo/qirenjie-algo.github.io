Abstract：The present application provides a lateral control method and apparatus, and a vehicle, which can be applied to the field of intelligent driving. The method comprises: acquiring a predicted trajectory of a vehicle and a boundary line of a first obstacle around the vehicle; determining a lateral collision risk between the vehicle and the first obstacle on the basis of the predicted trajectory and the boundary line; and when the lateral collision risk satisfies a first preset condition, controlling the vehicle to perform a lateral obstacle avoidance operation. The present application can be applied to an intelligent vehicle or an electric vehicle, and improves the lateral collision avoidance capability of the vehicle, thereby facilitating improving the driving and riding safety of a user.
